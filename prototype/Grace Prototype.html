Speak as Margaret

The page can’t use your microphone, so type what Margaret says. It plays as speech: her words fill Grace’s answer field, then send when she stops. Speaking while Grace talks interrupts her.

Try this

The tablet starts on its Home screen. 1. Tap Ask Grace in the rail (she opens full screen). 2. Ask her to pay your Chase bill. 3. Follow her on the page: tap the password field and type anything, tap Sign in, Pay card, Continue and Submit payment yourself. Grace points; she never taps for you. The browser’s ✕ closes it and goes to the Home screen; Home in the rail keeps it as it was. The rest of the tablet works too: Calendar, Email and Settings, Volume and Wi-Fi on the left, and asking Grace to change a setting, add an appointment, or find or reply to an email.
